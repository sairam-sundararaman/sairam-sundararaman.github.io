The Geometry of Bit-Collapse: what Int8 does to a loss landscape

Feb 2026 · 12 min read  optimizationquantization

Post-training quantization is sold as free compression. It isn't. I trained a ResNet-18, snapshotted the weights, quantized to Int8, and walked random directions through loss space to compare curvature before and after.

2. Curvature, measured

For a filter \(h\) along direction \(v\), the local curvature is the quadratic form of the Hessian:

$$\kappa(v) = v^{\top} \nabla^2_{\theta} \mathcal{L}(\hat\theta)\, v, \qquad \hat\theta = \arg\min_{\|\theta - \theta^\star\|_2 \le r} \mathcal{L}(\theta)$$

The top eigenvalue jumps by roughly an order of magnitude after quantization — the basin sharpens exactly where SGD struggles most.
