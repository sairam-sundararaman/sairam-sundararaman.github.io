Sairam Sundararaman
Privacy and robustness in machine learning, from first principles
AbstractPublicationsWritingProjectsRésumé
Abstract. The thread through my work is failure: take a method people rely on, then work out exactly when and why it breaks. A TMLR paper does this for the R-learner in network causal inference; a SAM reproduction does it for a popular optimizer; a bit-collapse study does it for post-training quantization. Currently at WSAI, IIT Madras, deriving noise calibration for differentially private LLM mechanisms by hand rather than treating it as a recipe.
Keywords — differential privacy · robustness · adversarial ML · optimization
