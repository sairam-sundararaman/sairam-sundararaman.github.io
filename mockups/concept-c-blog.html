Why your DP-SGD budget burns faster than you think

The moments accountant is honest. The intuition people carry away from it is not.

Everyone remembers \(O(\sqrt{T})\) composition. Fewer people notice that the square root lives in the conversion from Rényi divergence to approximate-DP — not in composition itself, which is plainly additive.

KEY INEQUALITY$$\rho_{\text{tot}} \;=\; T\cdot\Big(\tfrac{q^2}{2\sigma^2} + O(q^4)\Big),\qquad \varepsilon \;\le\; \rho_{\text{tot}} + \tfrac{\log(1/\delta)}{a-1}$$

The two-line audit

# analytic Gaussian mechanism — call this at EVERY checkpoint
def eps_now(T, q, sigma, delta):
    rho = T * q**2 / (2*sigma**2)
    return rho + 2*sigma*q*math.sqrt(rho*math.log(1/delta))
Rule of thumb — if early stopping is on the table, the planned horizon is the accounting horizon. Restarts silently re-spend the whole budget.

Budget in steps, never epochs. A restart mid-epoch has already spent its noise; the accountant knows this even when your config file doesn't.
